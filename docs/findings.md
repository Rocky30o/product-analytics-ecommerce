About 78% of users who viewed products never added anything to their cart,
and among those who did, 52% did not buy. Electronics converts best
(22% view-to-cart, 50% cart-to-purchase). Apparel is the biggest opportunity:
it has many viewers, but only 5.5% add to cart and 32.5% of those buy.
I would test size guides on apparel pages and cart reminders across the store.

Retention: Week-1 retention fell from 37% (cohort of 4 Nov) to 25% (11 Nov)
and 17% (18 Nov). The 4 Nov cohort is probably inflated because data starts
on 1 Nov, so some returning customers look like new users. Even so, the
downward trend suggests later-month visitors (possibly Black Friday shoppers)
were less likely to return. I would compare retention by acquisition type and
test a win-back email for first-time buyers.
